SAAMAN 1.3.22
Unload the previous DLL before loading SAAMAN.dll in every connected instance,
including the sequencer and proxy. Keep SAAMAN above TopSky in the plug-in list.

Fresh settings use shared vatIRIS mode and VATSIM live, connecting automatically
on load/login. Set both ARR and DEP runways in EuroScope before joining.
Backend: https://backend.vatiris.se
No separate vatIRIS login or controller key is needed. Local mode is secondary.

This release fixes individual inactive/departure-runway assignments, RNP text,
post-feeder reconnect/Recompute, line-0 cutoff and rounding, Information sizing,
coordination popups with direct/proxy connections, and recent direct-to timing
when EuroScope restores STAR points after a heading assignment.
No backend update is required. The DLL is identical to the live-tested test2.

Read docs/user-guide.html for the controller guide, docs/user-guide-wiki.html
for wiki-ready content, and docs/RELEASE-NOTES-1.3.22.md for release details.
